Both Duo screens, one URL

Closed, iPhone Duo is a 5.4 in cover at 466 by 678 points. Open, it is a 7.6 in canvas at 951 by 669. The crease is a 40-point band, not half the width. Paste a public URL. We put it in both frames.

How

1

Paste a public http or https URL. Ports 80 and 443 only. Private IPs are refused.

2

We fetch the HTML, pin a base href, and show it in two iframes sized to Duo CSS points.

3

Cover stays 466 by 678. Inner starts landscape 951 by 669. A 40-point crease sits on the fold.

Limits

  • 20 previews per IP per hour. 1 MB HTML cap. 10 second fetch.
  • This is not a CSS autopsy. Bare does that. Crease is two viewports.
  • Sites that fight bots, demand login, or break out of iframes will look empty or wrong. That is the fetch, not your layout.
  • Numbers follow the iPhone Duo simulator profile (points, not pixels). Do not type them into a Swift .frame.
